The games

In development · v1.1.4

Telecrisis Chris

A tactical turn-based space roguelike. Selecct a Pilot and neutralize each star's Core to prevent unstable Core's from killing innocent civillians. Core's obstacles, enemies and gravity will challenge you, with increasing difficulty at stars further from the Federation Base.

 In development · v0.16.1

Telecrisis Revenge

An arena tactics RPG with timed strikes. The Federation's has implemented gladiatorial games to appease the masses, embark on a quest to reach the pinnacle of glory and see what truths will be uncovered.

 Early development · v0.20.9

Telecrisis Emergence

The final game of the trilogy, in the cockpit. Escape in your parents' impounded starfighters and start the uprising.
