Telecrisis Revenge

The classes

Build a team of up to six. Weight sets armor, speed and matchups: heavy beats medium, medium beats light, light beats heavy. Each class has a passive, a signature technique and a mastery won in its class trial that upgrades every fighter of that class.

Vanguard

Tank · Heavy
Heavy, Blade

Holds the line in heavy plating. Slow, but very hard to hurt.

  • HP 24
  • Attack 4
  • Defense 2
  • Speed 3
  • Move 3
  • Dodge none
Challenger: +1 defense. Enemies that can reach the Vanguard must attack it
Signature · 2 energy
  • Shield Wall — Until your next turn, you and the allies next to you take half damage.
Mastery
  • Phalanx — allies next to a Vanguard take 15% less damage. Won in Vanguard Trial: Hold the Line (The Rim League).

Brawler

Hand-to-hand · Medium
Fist, Heavy

Fights with fists and powered gauntlets. Punishes anyone who swings at them.

  • HP 20
  • Attack 5
  • Defense 1
  • Speed 5
  • Move 4
  • Dodge 8%
Counterpuncher: parry counters hit twice as hard
Signature · 2 energy
  • Counter Stance — Until your next turn, hit back at every adjacent enemy that attacks you.
Mastery
  • Iron Fists — a normal guard against an adjacent attacker also counters for half. Won in Brawler Trial: Knuckle Pit (The Kessrow Circuit).

Breacher

Heavy hitter · Heavy
Heavy

Cracks armor and doors with breaching gear. Big single hits, slow feet.

  • HP 20
  • Attack 5
  • Defense 1
  • Speed 3
  • Move 3
  • Dodge 3%
Armor Breaker: ignores half of the target's Defense
Signature · 2 energy
  • Sunder — Weapon strike that exposes the target and knocks it back.
Mastery
  • Demolisher — ignores all of the target's Defense. Won in Breacher Trial: Armoured Wall (The Halcyon Games).

Infiltrator

Rogue / thief · Light
Blade, Pistol, Fist

Slips behind the enemy. Lifts credits off everyone they drop.

  • HP 16
  • Attack 4
  • Defense 0
  • Speed 8
  • Move 5
  • Dodge 15%
Backstab & Pickpocket: a further +15% damage from behind, +3 credits per takedown
Signature · 2 energy
  • Vanish — Free action. This turn you can move through enemies and your next attack counts as from behind.
Mastery
  • Ghost — Vanish costs 1 less and +1 move. Won in Infiltrator Trial: Shadow Bout (The Core Championship).

Ranger

Mobile ranged · Medium
Pistol, Rifle, Blade

Sidearms and rifles on the move. Scales terrain other fighters have to walk around.

  • HP 16
  • Attack 4
  • Defense 0
  • Speed 6
  • Move 4
  • Dodge 10%
Free Climber: +1 move; climbing costs no extra movement and can go up 2 levels in a step
Signature · 2 energy
  • Covering Fire — Light hit that suppresses the target: its next attack does half damage.
Mastery
  • Eagle Eye — +1 range with pistols and rifles. Won in Ranger Trial: High Ground (The Rim League).

Sniper

Long range · Light
Rifle, Pistol

Hits hard from far away, and harder from above. Weak when cornered.

  • HP 13
  • Attack 5
  • Defense 0
  • Speed 5
  • Move 3
  • Dodge 8%
Vantage: +15% more damage for each level above the target (+30% per level in all)
Signature · 1 energy
  • Mark Target — Free action, once a turn. The whole party deals 20% more damage to this enemy. You keep one mark at a time: marking another enemy moves it.
Mastery
  • Deadeye — Critical hits ignore Defense. Won in Sniper Trial: Long Sight (The Halcyon Games).

Medic

Healer · Medium
Kit, Pistol

Keeps the team standing, and the only one who can bring a fallen fighter back.

  • HP 15
  • Attack 2
  • Defense 0
  • Speed 5
  • Move 4
  • Dodge 5%
Triage: +3 to heals, and heals on an ally below half HP are 50% stronger
Signature · 4 energy
  • Revive — Brings a knocked-out ally back beside you at 40% HP.
Mastery
  • Second Wind — Revive costs 2 less and brings the ally back at 70% HP. Won in Medic Trial: Long Odds (The Kessrow Circuit).

Technician

Tech · Light
Tool, Kit, Pistol

The team's battery. Generates energy fast, feeds it to allies, and shuts enemies down with gadgets.

  • HP 15
  • Attack 4
  • Defense 0
  • Speed 6
  • Move 4
  • Dodge 6%
Overclock: gains 2 energy a turn instead of 1, and starts with 1
Signature · 1 energy
  • Hotwire — Give an adjacent ally 2 energy.
Mastery
  • Surge — Hotwire gives 3 energy instead of 2. Won in Technician Trial: Signal Jam (The Core Championship).

Telecrisis Revenge is in development, so classes and numbers may change. Stats shown are at level 1, before training and gear.
