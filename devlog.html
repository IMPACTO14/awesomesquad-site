Telecrisis Revenge

November 18, 2026 · v0.13.0

No two matches alike

  • Choose your path: each circuit now opens three matches at a time and one more with every win. The final needs the story match and most of the ladder, not every bout.
  • Start patterns: head-on, switched ends, corner to corner, pincer, split team, broadside, and crossfire for the story fights. Every attempt rolls a new one, and the briefing map shows it.
  • Entry rules: lightweights only, no guns, one of each class, bring a tank and more, on optional matches and side bouts. Your whole roster and your gear choices matter now.
  • A side-bout board of three generated bouts per circuit, from 1v1 duels to full squads, redrawn after every match.
  • Smarter Federation: four difficulties. On Standard and Hard, enemies flank, gang up on the wounded, go for knockouts and fall back when hurt.
  • Bonus objectives and medals (win fast, nobody knocked out, no supplies and more), injuries that sit knocked-out fighters out for a match, and challenge modifiers that make every match harder for more credits and XP.
  • Vantor Academy, the Federation's own gladiator school, turns up twice a circuit with named fighters, a grudge and trophies to win.
  • 35 achievements, ready for Steam.
November 17, 2026 · v0.12.0

The Games go pocket-sized

  • Play on your phone: open the game in your phone's browser, turn it sideways, and it switches to a phone layout with bigger buttons and text. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen like an app, updating itself when a new build is out.
  • Touch controls for every timed input: an on-screen pad appears only when a meter needs it: a direction pad, a big Tap / Hold / Mash button, and left and right buttons for Alternating Mash. Lock-On works like a trackpad: drag anywhere and the crosshair follows, so your finger never covers the target.
  • Timing that keeps up: every press is now judged at the moment your finger landed, not when the game got round to it. A new Timing check in Options has you tap along to a beat and adjusts for your screen's delay.
  • Battle on a small screen: Fighter / Info / Log tabs, tap any unit to inspect it, tap a tile twice to move, and tap any attack or supply to see what it does (and, if it's locked, why).
  • Finger scrolling for every list, the Team, Store and Records tabs as swipe strips, and option rows that grow to fit their text.
November 16, 2026 · v0.11.0

Engagement, dodging and a harder Federation

  • Squaring up: fighters next to each other face off. An engaged fighter keeps facing its opponent, so its allies can be flanked, and walking away gives the opponent a parting strike. Light fighters slip away free.
  • Dodging by class: quick fighters dodge some hits, but never a Critical. Land one on a dodge and it's DODGE DENIED!
  • Story / Standard / Hard enemy strength, a tougher baseline, and harder circuit finals and story matches, tuned with a campaign simulator.
  • Side bouts that matter: the ladder leaves you a few levels short of each final. Exhibitions and the Open Arena pay full XP and can drop gear and four new arena-only rare finds.
  • Gear: a Mk tier of gear for every circuit (77 pieces in all), rare finds from story matches and trials, selling, gear comparison in the store, and three new supplies.
  • No more misclicks: place your fighters before each match, see every attack's range, and confirm both the target and the risk choice. You can back out and get your energy back.
  • Defense rework: Defense now scales with the attacker's level, so armor and training matter all the way to level 80.
November 15, 2026 · v0.10.0

The Games open

  • The campaign: four circuits of proving grounds, cup matches, story matches and class trials, each ending in a gated final. The level cap is 99.
  • Sixteen timed inputs, from Sweep Bars to Lock-Ons, and the Training Grounds to practise them.
  • Match rules and hazards: hold the zone, survive, champions, blitz and iron rules; shock plates, debris, oil fires, cover and overwatch.
  • A crowd that backs the underdog.
  • The space-coliseum look: gladiator figures, an angled board that shows height, and a new arena backdrop.

Telecrisis Chris

November 18, 2026 · v1.1.0

Keystones, phone play and tougher late fights

  • Pilot keystones: every pilot has four rule-bending keystones. Pick one per run in the Pilot's Lounge. A banner and a pulse off your ship show when one fires.
  • Seven keystones that change how you fly: Recoil Rail, Anchor & Launch, Dogleg, Grapple Hook, Pinball, Hijack and Blink. Each is your choice to use, turn by turn. Insider Trading now lets you use the roll it shows or roll fresh.
  • Play on a phone or tablet: a UI Size setting, pinch to zoom, drag to look around, press and hold for details, and a full-screen button.
  • Tougher late fights: from tier 16, some stars bring Veteran Wing (fewer, much tougher enemies), Shield Drones (enemies with shields that recharge) or Hardened Hulls (every volley does 1 less), each with its own payoff.
  • Round boards everywhere: every star and side quest (except the Wormhole corridor) now plays on a circular board with a bigger edge.
  • Escort Convoy got sharper: raiders now go for the freighter, and it needs you close to survive.
  • Ace Hunt pays double, and the ace's escorts give you a turn to react before they open fire.
  • Salvo flies faster with a bigger blast, and the arming point is marked on the warning line.
  • One console layout across star fights, side quests and PvP, with a mission card for each side quest.
  • Side-quest energy is now banked for your next stop. Turrets can't spawn right on top of you.
  • Balance: Full Spread rolls a third of your dice at no more than 2 extra targets. Probe rolls only the zone's base dice. Critical Mass leaves you 10 energy for the shop. Schmeckeldorf gets +1 hull, LSP −1 shield.
  • The star map no longer overlaps its labels, and hovering the PAR and feats strip explains each bonus.
November 2, 2026 · v1.0.2

Lounge abilities and a cleaner cockpit

  • 40 new Lounge abilities mixed into the normal draw.
  • Core momentum is visible, with x2, x3 and x4 damage multipliers.
  • Star recap shows the Core, your bounty and which abilities fired.
  • Run variety: a smaller Garage with rarity, and a launch protocol to pick each run.
  • Menus and console: one layout in every mode, no scrolling menus, and a controller pass.
  • Salvo flies faster and checks its whole flight path for hits.
October 26, 2026 · v1.0.1

A real first run

  • New onboarding: tier 0 is a guided hunt (beacon, first shot, unlock banners, payoff), and you can skip it.
  • Progression: new pilots join as you clear tiers, and the run-end screen shows what unlocks next.
  • The Core: shots now chip its shields too, while DRAIN stays the fast way through.
  • Readability: text scales with your screen (Steam Deck included), and the console and side panels were reworked.
